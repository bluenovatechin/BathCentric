# BathCentric: update every image

One ready-to-copy prompt for **every image the website uses** (66 images), so you can replace them all with a matching set.

## How to use this file

1. Work through the sections **in order**: the most important (and most confusing) images come first.
2. Copy the whole block, set the **shape** shown in the section heading, generate, and keep the best result.
3. Save it with the **exact file name** (`.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`) into the `photos/` folder, replacing the old file.
4. Run `npm run dev` to check it. Tell me when a batch is done and I will check every image.

**Tips**
- If your tool has a separate negative-prompt box, paste the line starting with **Avoid:** there.
- The site trims a thin strip from one edge to remove generator marks such as ✦: the **right edge** of landscape images, the **bottom edge** of portrait and square ones. Keep important details away from that edge.
- For each enclosure type, check the result against its description below before you save it: count the glass panels, check the door type and check that every fitting has the same finish.

## The four enclosure types (built into every prompt)

These descriptions are the reason the old images were confusing, especially the L-Type. Every prompt that shows an enclosure uses the matching one word for word.

- **Single Panel:** A SINGLE PANEL shower enclosure: exactly ONE fixed frameless clear glass panel standing on the floor and meeting the back wall, NO door, the entry is completely open so you walk in beside the panel; one slim round support rod runs horizontally from the top edge of the glass to the side wall; small square L-connector clamps fix the glass edge to the back wall; a small U-shaped clamp holds the glass at the floor.
- **L-Type:** An L-TYPE corner shower enclosure: the shower sits in the corner of the room with two tiled walls behind it; glass closes the two open sides and the two glass sides meet at a 90 degree right angle in a square L shape, seen from the front at a slight angle so both glass sides are clearly visible; the FRONT side has a hinged glass door next to the wall, fixed with two square door hinges, then a fixed glass panel; the SIDE return is one fixed glass panel; a square L-connector clamp joins the two glass sides at the outer corner; a long vertical pull handle that doubles as a towel rod is on the door; one slim round support rod runs from the back wall to the top of the glass.
- **Sliding:** A SLIDING shower enclosure across an alcove between two side walls: one fixed frameless glass panel and one glass door that hangs from two round stainless steel sliding wheels running along a straight round top rod fixed from wall to wall; the door is slid partly open behind the fixed panel; a small round knob handle on the door; a small floor guide block keeps the bottom of the door in line; small square L-connector clamps fix the fixed panel to the wall; NO hinges, the door does not swing.
- **Swing:** A SWING shower enclosure across an alcove between two side walls: a straight run of glass made of a fixed glass panel, then a hinged glass door, then a fixed glass panel; the door is held by two square glass door hinges and is opened slightly outward towards the camera; a long vertical pull handle that doubles as a towel rod on the door; one slim round support rod from the top of the glass to the back wall; small square L-connector clamps fix the glass to the side walls.

| | Panels | Door | Moves how |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single Panel | 1 fixed panel | none, open entry | nothing moves |
| L-Type | front side (door + fixed panel) + side return panel, at 90° | hinged | swings out |
| Sliding | 1 fixed panel + 1 sliding door, straight line | sliding | slides behind the fixed panel |
| Swing | fixed panel + door + fixed panel, straight line | hinged | swings out |
